# PropertyBot (Web) — Agent Quick Start

A one-pager you can forward to agents. For the technical setup, see the main
[`README.md`](../README.md).

## What it is
Arise Real Estate's internal AI assistant, now on the **web** (works on phone and
desktop). Ask about any project and get instant briefings — **every answer ends
with clickable Source links** you can open and share with clients.

## How to get in
1. Open the link your manager shared (add it to your phone's Home Screen for an
   app-like shortcut — your browser's "Add to Home Screen" / "Install").
2. Enter the **staff passcode**. Internal use only — don't share the link.

## How to ask
Type naturally, in **English, Bahasa Malaysia, or 中文** — it replies in your
language. Examples:
- `Brief me on KSL Riverhaus`
- `What's the sales package for Avenia?`
- `Compare Macrolink Medini vs Isola Coast Phase 2 for a Singaporean investor`
- `Which banks finance Kews Green?`

## The one rule about Source links
Every answer finishes with a **Sources** section:
- **Price List / Floor Plan / Brochure / Photos** links → open these and share
  with clients. ✅
- **"— internal record"** link → the Notion record, for **managers only** (won't
  open for everyone). ℹ️

If a project shows no asset links, tell your manager to add the Price List /
Floor Plan URLs in Notion so the bot can surface them.

## Good habits
- **Verify figures** before sending to clients — the bot is an assistant, you're
  the professional.
- Don't paste clients' IC / bank / passport details into the bot.
- Bot says it doesn't know a project? It may not be in the knowledge base yet —
  ask your manager to add it and re-run the sync.
